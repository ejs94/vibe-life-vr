# Assets

The VR overlay lives in `assets/valve_vr/`. It is deployed as a `valve_vr`
overlay above the read-only `valve` game data. The engine still launches the
base `valve` gamedir, so deploy the same files to both `out/valve/models` and
`out/valve_vr/models` when testing.

## Ownership rules

- The baked viewmodel hand is authoritative when a weapon has one.
- The standalone hand is for unarmed presence and a free offhand. It is the
  HEV glove from Half-Life: VR, so it matches the gloves baked into the adopted
  weapon viewmodels.
- Never generate a detailed hand from primitives.
- Never reshape a glove by displacing vertices. Fix the source mesh or the
  animation instead.
- Do not add bones to viewmodels. Append attachments at the end of the table.
- Do not change the authoritative firing line. Hand and aim calibration is
  `vuser2`; it is not a ballistics override.
- Do not ship an asset without a labeled comparison sheet approved by the
  maintainer.

## Viewmodels

The MMOD reanimation pack provides 15 viewmodels under
`assets/valve_vr/models/`. The pack's provenance, download source, archive
checksum and per-weapon audit live in
[`assets/valve_vr/CREDITS.md`](../../assets/valve_vr/CREDITS.md).

`vr_weapons.json` is the declarative weapon profile. It carries grip offsets,
grip rotations, muzzle fallbacks, two-handed gating and reload metadata. The
profile is hot-reloadable with `vr_reload_weapon_profiles`.

Grip invariants:

- `XR_GetWeaponPose()` is the single source of truth for the renderer, the
  viewmodel and the muzzle fallback.
- `grip_offset` is a per-weapon model-space socket and is not mirrored for
  left-handed players. Only the global `vr_weapon_offset_right` is mirrored.
- The crossbow `grip` bone is the front/foregrip. The main grip comes from the
  right-hand bone.
- The anchor uses the viewmodel grip bone aligned to the palm. `grip_offset` is
  only a fallback for models without a grip bone or attachment.

## Standalone hand

`assets/valve_vr/models/v_hand.mdl` is the standalone two-hand model.

- HEV glove extracted from Half-Life: VR's `v_hand_hevsuit.mdl`.
- 35-bone contract following the Valve naming convention.
- Bodygroup 0 draws the right chain; bodygroup 3 draws the left chain.
- The palm sits at the model origin; fingers point along `+X`, palms face
  inward, thumbs up along `+Z`.
- The left chain is mirrored from the right across the authored X axis, which
  flips the palm normal and keeps the fingers along the same axis. Reflection
  across Y would reverse the fingers and is wrong.
- It is generated by `scripts/build_hev_twohand_mdl.py` and validated by
  `scripts/check_hand_asset.py`. Do not hand-edit the binary.

## Asset audit pipeline

These are diagnostic tools, not gates. `A1` was retired on 2026-11-09; their
output is working evidence, never a gate result.

1. Decompile the model with `scripts/mdldec.sh` into `out/mmod_import/`.
2. Audit boundary loops, cards, non-manifold edges and physical defects:

   ```sh
   python3 scripts/audit_holes.py \
       --model out/mmod_import/decompiled/v_9mmhandgun \
       --out out/qa/V1/asset_diagnostics
   ```

   The run writes `glock_holes.json`, `glock_defects.csv`,
   `glock_loop_sheet.png`, `glock_body_sheet.png` and the baseline score.
   `asset_verdict` is separate from any gate status.

3. Audit materials, UVs, normals and topology:

   ```sh
   python3 scripts/audit_viewmodel_assets.py \
       --decompiled-dir out/mmod_import/decompiled \
       --out out/qa/V1/asset_diagnostics/asset_audit
   ```

4. Prove the compiler roundtrip before editing:

   ```sh
   scripts/check_mdl_roundtrip.sh assets/valve_vr/models/v_crossbow.mdl
   ```

5. Render a 360-degree geometry sheet:

   ```sh
   python3 scripts/render_mdl_360.py \
       --qc out/mmod_import/decompiled/<model>/<model>.qc \
       --out out/qa/A4/latest/<model>_360
   ```

6. Compare the result in-engine with a fixed camera and a labeled sheet.

## Hole repair

The 1998 viewmodels are hollow shells. Some boundary loops are intentional
(barrel mouths, magwells, ejection ports); others are missing receiver, stock
or handguard faces that become obvious in VR.

The repair pipeline is:

1. Find welded boundary loops and non-manifold edges with `audit_holes.py`.
2. Group them into physical defects and classify each loop as intentional,
   accidental or uncertain. Do not cap a barrel, magwell or ejection port.
3. For an accidental hole, duplicate the boundary loop, offset the new loop
   inward along the local normal, and bridge the two loops. This creates a
   visible inner liner without changing the outer silhouette.
4. For a tear, stitch the sheets that share the non-manifold vertices; do not
   cap the opening. The Glock pilot has `body_tear_1` and
   `clip_loop_1_open_side`.
5. Preserve UVs, weights, bones, sequences, events, bodygroups and attachments.
6. Re-run the roundtrip, audit, 360 render and Monado suite.
7. Pilot on the Glock, then the crossbow, before batch repair.

Do not fill a hole by moving existing vertices across the opening. That changes
the silhouette and usually breaks the UVs.

## MMOD baked hand extraction

The MMOD hand's grip shape is authored in the mesh, not in its finger
rotations. The extraction path is:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_mmod_standalone_hand.py \
    --input out/mmod_import/decompiled/<model>/HEV_hands_reference.smd \
    --output out/mmod_standalone

python3 scripts/check_hand_asset.py \
    --model out/mmod_standalone/v_hand.mdl --grip
```

The extractor keeps weapon-space orientation, puts the wrist at the origin,
splits right and left triangles into `rhand`/`lhand`, and merges the extra thumb
and upper-arm bones into the 35-bone contract. Per-weapon extraction is
preferred over reshaping a shared hand.

## Deployment

```sh
./scripts/deploy_quest_assets.sh valve /sdcard/xash
```

For desktop tests, copy the same model and `vr_weapons.json` to both
`out/valve/models` and `out/valve_vr/models`. Confirm in the engine log which
file was loaded; the overlay trap is a common cause of "my asset did not
change".
